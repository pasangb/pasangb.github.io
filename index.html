01 / The traveler behind the toolkit

Curiosity is my
favorite starting point.

I'm pursuing a B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at New York University, graduating in May 2028, with a 3.7 GPA. I'm interested in hardware engineering and want to learn more about chip design, digital logic, and how hardware is described and verified.

I'm getting into low-level programming and hardware description languages such as SystemVerilog. My learning experiments help me connect code with digital hardware behavior. Alongside that, I've built a solar-powered vehicle, a posture monitor, and power electronics for ARC Robotics VIP, and I help students troubleshoot as an engineering teaching assistant.

Away from the workbench, I enjoy hiking, taking photographs, and exploring new places. I bring that same curiosity to engineering: trying things, asking questions, and learning along the way.
